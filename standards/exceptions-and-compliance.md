# Exceptions and compliance

Defines how compliance is assessed and how intentional exceptions are recorded,
reviewed, approved, and retired. It does not contain implementation tutorials or
application-specific audit findings; those belong in [guides](../guides/README.md)
and [audits](../audits/) respectively.
